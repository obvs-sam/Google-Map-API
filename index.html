How to Install (Step-by-Step)

1

Accept tester invite

Open the invite email sent by Firebase and sign in using the Google account that received the invite.

2

Tap Download

Click "Download App" here or in the invite email to open the Firebase distribution page for the latest build.

3

Allow installation

Follow the prompts. If the Firebase Tester app is suggested, allow it for easier install and updates.

4

Handle Play Protect

If Play Protect shows a caution, confirm you trust the invited build and continue—this build is release-signed.

5

Open & test

Open the app from the app drawer or tap "Open App". Test the features and report any issues to the dev team.

Quick tips

Uninstall any previously installed debug builds (if present) to avoid install conflicts before installing this build.

Need help?

Send your device model, Android version, and a screenshot of the problem to the dev team for faster debugging.
